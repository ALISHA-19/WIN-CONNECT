The journey, step by step

What a new woman leader sees on her first visit

WEBSITEWorks wellShe lands on womeninspiringnetwork.com

"Join WIN Connect, our AI-powered opportunity app" sits right at the top, with Davos 2027 registration, the podcast and 40+ panel videos. Press logos (Times of India, Vogue, Hindustan Times, Business Today) build trust fast.

JOINConfusingShe looks for how to join

She sees several calls to action: the "Join Now" app button, an "Are you a woman leader?" form and newsletter sign-ups. Each has its own purpose, but a newcomer can't easily tell which one makes her a member.

SIGN-INDrop-off riskShe clicks "Join Now" and sees only a sign-in button

A welcome line, "Connect. Collaborate. Thrive." and one button, Continue with LinkedIn. Nothing says what's inside, who's there, what it costs or what happens to her data.

INSIDEStrong productIf she gets in, a lot is built already

From the app's own labels: Compass search, WIN Circles, Opportunities, Events with QR check-in, messages, and three plans: WIN Club, Elite and Lumina.

SWOT, in plain words

Where WIN stands today

Strengths and weaknesses are things inside WIN's control. Opportunities and threats are what's happening outside.

Helps WINHolds WIN back
S

Strengths

What WIN is already good at
A name people trust.Presence at Davos, Cannes, Climate Week and UNGA week does what no ad budget can.
A founder who knows storytelling.23 years running a PR firm means press, partners and speakers come easily.
A ready content library.40+ panel videos and the WIN Voices podcast are content most apps would have to create from scratch.
A product with real depth.Search, Circles, Opportunities, Events, messaging and paid plans already exist.
LinkedIn sign-in.Joining is one tap, and every member is linked to a real professional profile, so fake accounts are harder to create.
W

Weaknesses

What's holding it back
The first screen doesn't explain itself.The sign-in page doesn't yet say what WIN Connect is or why to join.
LinkedIn is the only way in, for now.Women who don't use LinkedIn, or don't want to link it, can't join yet. The code already has Google and Apple sign-in buttons written, so this looks like it's on the way.
Several calls to action.The app button, a Google Form and newsletter sign-ups each have a purpose, but a newcomer can't tell which one makes her a member.
Shared links look bare.The web app link shows only a title, with no description or image, on WhatsApp or LinkedIn.
Life happens around events.Energy peaks in Davos week, then quiets down. An app needs a reason to come back on a normal Tuesday.
O

Opportunities

Where WIN can grow
Make the app the "after-party" of every event.The app already has QR check-in for events. Add "who you met" and follow-ups, and every lounge keeps paying off after the day ends. Davos 2027 is the perfect first test.
Turn speakers into searchable members."Show me who spoke on gender-lens investing at Davos" becomes a real Compass search.
A clear ladder of paid plans.Club, Elite and Lumina can each promise something specific: access, intros, invitations.
Corporate and partner memberships.Organisations WIN already works with, such as Invest India, CII and the Cherie Blair Foundation for Women, could sponsor places for their women leaders.
Funding matchmaking.The app already captures funding asks from $50K to $50M+, which family offices and foundations want to browse.
T

Threats

What could hurt WIN
Women already have networks.LinkedIn, Lean In Circles, Chief and WhatsApp groups compete for the same evening hour.
Senior women's time is precious.A few weak or spammy AI intros, and top members go quiet. Once they leave, the network loses its pull.
Data and privacy rules.AI drafting intros from profile data has to follow India's DPDP Act and Europe's GDPR, with clear consent.
Relying on LinkedIn.If LinkedIn changes its sign-in rules, WIN's only front door closes.
Mission vs money.WIN is a not-for-profit movement that now has paid plans. The story has to explain why both can be true.
The one-line diagnosis

The product is strong. Visitors just can't see it before they sign in.

WIN Connect is weeks old and already has search, circles, opportunities, events, chat and paid plans. The fastest win is not building more. It is showing visitors what's inside, so the right women walk in.

The single biggest improvement

Open the front door before asking her to sign in

Today the app asks for trust first and explains later. I'd flip that order. Here's a rough sketch of the same phone screen, today and after.

If I join

My first 90 days as WIN Connect's Product Manager

Listen first, fix what's obvious, then build the one loop that matters most: event, then app, then introduction, then a real business outcome.

DAYS 01–30

Listen and learn

  • Sit with you to agree on the one thing WIN Connect must do for members this year.
  • Talk to 15 members across Club, Elite and Lumina, plus 5 who signed up and went quiet.
  • Meet Sales, Marketing, Support and Ops, and put everything they ask for into one backlog.
  • Get tracking working so we have honest numbers.
  • Agree the small fixes with each team and ship them.

You get: a one-page product goal, a ranked backlog and a baseline of today's numbers.

DAYS 31–60

Open the door

  • Launch the public landing page and one clear main join button.
  • Finish Google and Apple sign-in, with a check on new accounts, plus the first-week welcome.
  • Build and test the Davos features (who's going, who you met, follow-ups) so they're ready by early January.
  • Add a one-tap "was this useful?" after every suggested connection.
  • Settle a steady sprint rhythm, with clear user stories and acceptance checks the team agrees on.

You get: more visitors becoming members, and more members active in week one.

DAYS 61–90

Win Davos 2027

  • Run the Davos Lounge on 21 January through the app: the existing QR check-in, then who-you-met and follow-ups.
  • Put speaker profiles in Compass.
  • Test the plan ladder with real members before pricing at scale.
  • Send a monthly one-page update on what we shipped, what moved and what's next.

You get: proof that event energy turns into lasting connections, and a story for partners and funders.

How we'd know it's working

One number to steer by

North Star

Useful connections made each month

An introduction that both women accepted, and at least one said was useful. It measures exactly what WIN promises, the right woman meeting the right woman. It's hard to fake with clicks or sign-ups.
